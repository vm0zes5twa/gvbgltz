# Watch the 'Survivor' Contestants Learn That Every Eliminated Person Is Still in the Game

Big things are going down on this week's episode of Survivor. As if the merge weren't a big deal on its own, every contestant is also about to learn that their eliminated peers never actually left the game.

On Monday, CBS released a sneak peek of Wednesday's episode—and it gives you a good look at how the unsuspecting Survivor contestants react to this season's massive "Edge of Extinction" twist.

The scene begins like every other Survivor merge, with Jeff Probst telling everyone to drop their buffs. But as everyone opens up their new red merge buffs, Probst drops one hell of a Survivor bomb—one that we, the viewers, knew about already, but that the contestants sure didn't see coming.

"Over the years, certain moments on Survivor have become iconic," Probst says. "Some moments take years to become iconic. Others become iconic the moment they happen.

"This might be one of them."

And then—as everyone looks terrified—Probst yells: "COME ON IN!"

That's when the six Edge of Extinction contestants—Reem, Keith, Chris, Rick ("Devens"), Aubry, and Wendy—march out of the trees. Understandably, everyone freaks out; imagine what it would feel like to blindside someone and vote them out, only to learn you're going to have to play with them again.
