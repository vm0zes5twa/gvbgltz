What are the most effective medicines for treating period cramps?

Naproxen and ibuprofen are among the most effective over-the-counter medicines for period cramps because they lower menstrual prostaglandins—the compounds responsible for uterine contractions and pain. Acetaminophen (paracetamol) is another option that can help relieve mild to moderate menstrual discomfort, though it doesn't target prostaglandins directly. That said, not all period pain is the same, and severe or worsening cramps can sometimes signal underlying conditions like endometriosis, fibroids, or PCOS. What are the most effective treatments available for ankle pain?

Ankle pain treatment depends on the cause, but the most effective approach for common injuries like sprains is the RICE method: **Rest, Ice, Compression, and Elevation**.
