How can I increase brown fat in my body to aid in weight management?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Brown fat, also known as brown adipose tissue, is a type of fat that burns calories to generate heat, helping regulate body temperature and support weight management. You can naturally increase brown fat activity through several evidence-based methods:

Cold exposure: Regularly spending time in cool environments (around 66°F/19°C), taking cold showers, or using ice packs can stimulate brown fat activation.
