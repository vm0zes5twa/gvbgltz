What is the definition of insomnia and how is it diagnosed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Insomnia is a common sleep disorder characterized by difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking up too early and being unable to return to sleep. It is typically diagnosed by evaluating a person's sleep patterns, medical history, and how poor sleep impacts daily functioning such as energy, mood, focus, and overall health.

Because insomnia can stem from many underlying causes—ranging from stress and anxiety to medical conditions or medication side effects—understanding the root of your symptoms is the first step toward better sleep.
