What is vitamin B12 IV treatment, and what are its benefits?

As the name implies, vitamin B12 IV treatment is an intravenous delivery of high-dose vitamin B12. Vitamin B12 IVs and injections are the most efficient and effective methods of vitamin B12 supplementation. This is because the IV goes directly into your bloodstream, and the shots are injected into your muscle. Vitamin B12 that you get through food, drinks, and oral supplements is not as concentrated and has to be digested before it can have any effect, and it can take up to 12 hours for digestion to be complete.

Insufficient levels of vitamin B12 can cause anemia. This condition makes it difficult for enough oxygen to travel through your body because your red blood cells are not healthy enough to produce enough hemoglobin. Hemoglobin is a protein in your red blood cells responsible for delivering oxygen to your organs and tissues and returning carbon dioxide to your lungs from organs and tissues.

In-home treatments vs. IV bars

IV bars are where you can hang out and socialize with other people who are also getting IV therapy. An IV bar might be preferable if you and a group of friends get IV therapy.

In-home treatments are more lowkey and relaxed. A medical professional will come to your home and administer the IV treatment for you. This is a good option if you would rather watch TV or read while you receive treatment.
