How do ultra-processed foods contribute to weight gain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ultra-processed foods are strongly linked to weight gain and obesity. These foods—such as packaged snacks, sugary drinks, and fast food—are engineered to be hyper-palatable, low in fiber, and high in added sugars, refined fats, and sodium. Studies show people eat significantly more calories per day when their diet is rich in ultra-processed foods, often without feeling full. Over time, this excess calorie intake, combined with disrupted hunger signals and blood sugar spikes, promotes fat storage and increases the risk of obesity, type 2 diabetes, and heart disease.
