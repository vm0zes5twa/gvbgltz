The Gamer's Neck Fix: Using RLT for Long Sessions

Neck stiffness is one of the most common complaints among gamers, especially after marathon sessions. Forward head posture, prolonged screen time and minimal movement strain the muscles and joints of your cervical spine. Red light therapy (RLT) can be a game-changer, offering a non-invasive, drug-free way to ease stiffness, promote recovery and boost comfort during long gaming sessions.
