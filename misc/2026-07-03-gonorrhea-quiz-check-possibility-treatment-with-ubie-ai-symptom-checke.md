What is Gonorrhea?

Gonorrhea is a sexually transmitted disease caused by the N. gonorrhoeae bacteria.

Typical Symptoms of Gonorrhea

Burning or painful urination with reduced urine volume

Pus comes out from urethra - the opening when urine exits from

Redness or swelling of the urethra - the opening where urine exits from

Urethral itching or discomfort

Pain at the start of passing urine

Pain or burning during urination

Abnormal color, amount or odor of vaginal discharge

Increase in vaginal discharge

Diagnostic Questions for Gonorrhea

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you experienced burning or painful urination along with a decrease in urine volume?

Is there pus coming out of your urethra?

Is there redness or swelling around the urethral opening?

Have you been experiencing any itching or discomfort in your urethra?

Do you feel pain when you start peeing?

Treatment of Gonorrhea

Gonorrhea is treated with antibiotics, typically a combination of a single injection at the clinic and oral medications taken at home. It's crucial to avoid sex during treatment and ensure to complete the full course as advised.
