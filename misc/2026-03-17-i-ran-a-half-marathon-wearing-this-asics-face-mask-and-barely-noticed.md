The face cover goes beyond your basic two-loop mask. It was designed at the ASICS Institute of Sport Science (ISS), and the company says it "helps prevent the spread of droplets by covering the nasal passage without restricting airflow." To help wearers breathe easier, the bottom of the mask features air holes "that increase airflow while preventing saliva from splashing into the open air." Asics hasn't provided any data to prove how effective the face covering is at this goal.

ASICS Runners Face Cover

The key to the Asics mask is its superior comfort and stability compared to anything else I've put on my face. That in part comes from its shape—unlike other masks I've worn, this one has a semi-rigid shape to sit away from your mouth, so you have some more room to breath (and plenty of room for my ridiculous lockdown beard). That said, the material isn't stiff, and it repels water and dries quickly, making it easy to wash between uses. As a runner, I appreciate that feature for more than just avoiding sweat saturation. On one hot run early in the pandemic while wearing a neck gaiter covering, I poured water over my head to cool off, then felt as if I was drowning as the oversaturated material was sucked straight into my mouth when I tried to breathe. That doesn't happen with the Asics mask.

The mask's design makes it easy for just about anyone to wear, too. The straps are stretchy, with a with an adjustable drawcord, and it wraps all the way around your head to keep the fit tight and firm. When you're moving around a ton, that's a much preferable setup than the standard one-size loops that wrap around the ears.

[Half Marathon Wearing](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/)

I love the Asics mask, but I generally only wear it for workouts. Given the firm seal, it can be less comfortable than looser options over an extended period of time, and it takes an extra second to put on and take off given its strap setup. The only other knock against the mask is the price, at $40. If you've gotten used to cheap, disposable masks or utilitarian neck gaiters, you might not be willing to shell out for something that costs much more than a few bucks.

But if you're as dead set on training safely for the remainder of the pandemic (which, let's be honest, is not ending anytime soon), this mask is a sound investment to help to keep the spread down while you sweat.
