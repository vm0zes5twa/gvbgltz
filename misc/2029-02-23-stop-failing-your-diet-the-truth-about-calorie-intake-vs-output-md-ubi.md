Explanation

Weight management involves balancing the calories you eat with the calories your body uses. This is often summarized as 'calories in, calories out.' When you eat more calories than your body uses, the extra energy is stored as fat, leading to weight gain. Conversely, using more calories than you consume can lead to weight loss.

Key Points:

Calorie Balance: Maintaining a balance between calorie intake and output is crucial. If you consistently eat more than you burn, you may gain weight.

Self-Reported Intake: People often misreport or underestimate the number of calories they consume, which can affect weight management efforts.

Appropriate Feeding: It's important to ensure that calorie intake matches the body's needs, which can vary based on factors like activity level and metabolism.
