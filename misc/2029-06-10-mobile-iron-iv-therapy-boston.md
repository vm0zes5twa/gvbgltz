Iron IV therapy is a type of infusion that sends iron directly into your bloodstream. Traditional oral iron supplements pass through your digestive system, which can slow the absorption rate. At Drip Hydration, we use Venofer Iron IV to improve absorption, and you can have one of our licensed nurses administer the mobile iron IV drip in your hotel room, home or workspace.

What are the benefits of Iron IV therapy?

After you complete your iron IV therapy with a nurse from Drip Hydration, you could notice increased exercise tolerance and enhanced cognitive functioning. Stronger hair and nails, along with a more robust immune system, are additional benefits of iron IV therapy. Since iron helps to improve oxygen transport throughout your body, you might also notice you feel more energized after your session.

What can Iron IV therapy help with?

Most people choose our in-home iron IV therapy services to address symptoms associated with iron deficiency. For instance, this form of IV therapy is frequently used to manage low energy, headaches, pale skin and dizziness. You might also use this type of therapy to address restless leg syndrome. Because it is absorbed faster, IV iron therapy helps restore iron levels and correct anemia more quickly than oral supplements, which take longer to be absorbed.

Does Iron IV therapy have side effects?

Supplementing with iron often doesn’t cause any noticeable side effects. However, you may experience temporary effects from the infusion, such as dizziness, nausea or discomfort. Discussing your risk of having severe side effects, such as an allergic reaction, with your physician can help you feel more confident beginning your treatment. We also ensure that a medical professional administers your infusion and stays by your side throughout your at-home session.

Is Iron IV safe in pregnancy?

People with anemia might need IV iron treatment during pregnancy to correct the deficiency. Iron supplementation is safe during pregnancy, but it is important to seek approval from your primary care provider or OBGYN. You’ll also want to let our nurse know if you are pregnant when you schedule your appointment.

How long does Iron IV therapy last?

Treatment results can vary based on your starting iron levels and any health conditions you have. Many people have iron stores that stay elevated for several months. It’s best to get your iron levels tested every few months to determine when you need follow-up treatments.
