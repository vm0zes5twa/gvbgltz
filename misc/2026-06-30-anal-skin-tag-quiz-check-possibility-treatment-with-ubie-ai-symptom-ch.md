What is Anal Skin Tag?

This condition involves small growths of excess skin that can occur around the anus, primarily due to friction, hemorrhoids or anal fissures.

Typical Symptoms of Anal Skin Tag

Itching around the anus

Hemorrhoids or lumps around the anus

Feeling that my uterus has dropped down, or something is coming out of the vagina

Have given birth at least once before

Diagnostic Questions for Anal Skin Tag

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have anal itching?

Do you have hemorrhoids or lumps around your anus?

Do you feel your uterus dropping or something protruding from your vagina?

Have you given birth?

Treatment of Anal Skin Tag

A minor procedure can remove it. Treating the cause, such as anal skin tears or piles, can prevent skin tags from recurring.
