To prevent diarrhea from worsening, avoid these three types of foods:

Sugary foods (candy, soda, sweetened juices) — excess sugar draws water into the intestines, loosening stools.

High-fat foods (fried foods, greasy meats, heavy cream sauces) — fats are harder to digest and can accelerate bowel movements.

Spicy foods (hot peppers, chili, strong seasonings) — spices irritate the digestive tract and can intensify symptoms.

Sticking to bland, low-fat, low-sugar options like bananas, rice, toast, and broth can help your gut recover faster.

Because diarrhea can stem from many different causes — from mild dietary triggers to infections or underlying conditions — dietary changes alone may not be enough.
