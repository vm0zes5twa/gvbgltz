# This Total-Body, Muscle-Building Dumbbell Workout Takes Just 25 Minutes

[Workout Takes Just](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/)

Forget kettlebells, medicine balls, and ropes. The no-nonsense path to muscle starts with dumbbells. Think about it: Even mighty Peloton integrates dumbbells for strength building.

You’ll rely on them during this workout, a four-day-a-week total-body session that’ll add strength, boost endurance, and blast fat in 25 minutes a day.

Directions

Do this workout up to 6 days a week, aiming for at least 1 rest day. On days you don’t train, stay active by taking a 20-minute run.

Begin every workout with 3 rounds of this warmup: 5 Superman holds, 5 alternating reverse lunges, a 30-second plank, and 10 jumping jacks. Then get to work.

The Total-Body Dumbbell Workout

Do this workout as a 3-round circuit, using medium-weight dumbbells. Rest 30 seconds between each exercise.

1. Dumbbell Deadlift

[Just Minutes](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Place 2 dumbbells at the sides of your feet. Hinge at the waist, bend your knees, and grasp them, keeping your core tight. Stand, pulling the dumbbells off the floor and squeezing your glutes. Return the dumbbells to the floor. That’s 1 rep; do 10.

[Dumbbell Workout Takes Just](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/8753085427.html)

Eb says: “Focus on keeping your shoulders above your hips at all times. Your goal is to feel this in your hamstrings and glutes, not your lower back.”

2. Dumbbell Circus Ground-to-Overhead

Stand with a single dumbbell between your feet and grasp it with both hands. Tighten your hamstrings and your core, dropping into a low squat. Stand explosively (you may rise onto your toes as you do so) and pull the dumbbell up with both hands. As the weight reaches shoulder level, shift it to your right hand and drive it overhead. Lower it to your shoulder, then return it to the floor with both hands. That’s 1 rep. Repeat on the other side. Do 20 total reps.

3. Paused Dumbbell Close-Grip Press

Lie with your back on a bench, feet on the floor, glutes and abs tight, dumbbells held directly over your shoulders. Bend your elbows and shoulders, keeping your elbows close to your body as you lower the dumbbells to your ribcage. Pause for 1 second, then press them upward. That’s 1 rep; do 8 to 10.

4. Dumbbell Z-Press

Sit on the floor, legs straight, abs tight, dumbbells held at your shoulders. Without arching your back, straighten your arms, driving the dumbbells overhead. Slowly return them to your shoulders. That’s 1 rep; do 10 to 12.

Eb says: “Don’t flare your elbows out; keep them slightly in front of you, like our model. It’s a safer pressing angle for long-term shoulder health.”

The Finisher

Set a timer for 5 minutes. Repeat the moves in order, working to complete as many rounds as possible. Rest as needed, but keep your rest periods short.

5. Overhead Carry

[Just Minutes](https://peplawllc.com/)

Stand holding medium-weight dumbbells overhead, abs and glutes tight, rib cage in, and arms straight. Walk forward 20 steps. In tight confines? Walk ahead as far as you can, then turn back and walk to the start, aiming for 20 total steps.

Eb says: “Dealing with shoulder issues? Instead of holding the dumb-bells directly overhead, hold them at your shoulders.”

6. Farmer’s Carry to Farmer’s Hold

Stand holding the dumbbells at your sides, core and glutes tight, shoulder blades squeezed. Walk forward 20 steps, aiming to keep your hips and shoulders square. (If you don’t have room, walk back and forth for 20 total steps.) After you finish walking, stand, holding the dumbbells at your sides, for 20 seconds.
