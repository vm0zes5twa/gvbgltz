# Drake Is Now So Jacked You Can See His Six-Pack Abs Through His Shirt

Drake isn't slowing down after a recently knee injury — and he has the gym selfies to prove it. On Jan. 12, the rapper, singer, and actor posted a series of images and videos to his Instagram story showing him hitting the gym two and a half months after knee surgery. In one of the videos, he even lifts weights while doing squats, which is pretty impressive given the short time since his procedure.

On one of the photos, Drake included text reading,"10 weeks post op, I'm grinding for recovery EVERYDAY." While Drake's motivation is certainly impressive, some media outlets were quick to note that fans were more, ahem, focused on something else rather than mere workout gains. Let's just say more was "bulging" in the picture besides Drake's biceps, leading to colorful (and thirsty) fan commentary.

In late October 2020, Drake first posted a picture of his injured knee on Instagram. Though he hasn't been shy about talking about his recovery, it still remains unclear how exactly he sustained the injury in the first place.

"I actually want to use this post to send love and motivation to anybody else that 2020 let loose on," he posted at the time, according to People. "Start writing the best bounce back story now."
