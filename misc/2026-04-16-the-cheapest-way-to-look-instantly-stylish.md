# The Cheapest Way to Look Instantly Stylish

In a sea of business-casual khakis and button downs, eye-catching socks are a simple and affordable way to stand out among the masses.

But you don’t have to get dressed for the office to make this tried-and-true style staple work hard for you.

The right pair of socks can add interest and brightness to any outfit, and it usually won’t cost you more than a few bucks.

Socks ‘N’ Roll

To pull off the look like fashion blogger Isaac Hindin-Miller, start with a pair of slim-fit denim that’s easy to cuff. (Check out The Best Jeans Under $100.) Begin by rolling your hem twice: Pull the bottom of the pant up about four inches, and then fold that in half. Play with the amount of rolls to show as much or as little sock as you want.

[The Cheapest Way to](https://allpepay.com/blog/2225991164.html)

The color scheme here is easy to mimic—you can’t go wrong with black denim and royal blue socks.

When you keep the rest of your outfit simple, your socks act as the standout detail.

Once you’re comfortable showing off those mid-rise socks, explore new colors and patterns. Start with a four-pack of Bombas Marls ($46 for 4, bombas.com) or these bold printed florals from Stance ($15, stance.com).

To complete this look, just pair your denim with a black-and-white graphic tee and cool leather jacket. When the temperature drops, throw on that gray hoodie between your tee and jacket for a laidback, layered look.
