What Causes Allergies?

Whether you are having a reaction to springtime pollen or your best friend’s cat, all allergies work in much the same way: your immune system responds to something in your environment that is not actually hazardous to your health.

Treating Allergies at Home

Most of the time, allergies typically resolve on their own within a few hours of exposure to the allergen. Over-the-counter medications such as antihistamines can help alleviate symptoms of seasonal allergies while you wait. If you have a skin irritation, a topical corticosteroid cream may relieve swelling and itching.
