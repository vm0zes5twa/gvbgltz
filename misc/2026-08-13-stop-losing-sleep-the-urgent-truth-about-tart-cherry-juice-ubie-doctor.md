Natural Melatonin: Does Tart Cherry Juice Really Work?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Does tart cherry juice help with sleep? Yes, tart cherry juice contains natural melatonin and antioxidants. Short-term studies show it modestly increases total sleep time, improves sleep efficiency, and raises melatonin levels. The typical dose is 8 ounces twice daily for one to two weeks, though results vary by individual.

Key considerations include sugar content, potential medication interactions, long-term safety, and optimal dosing (see details below).

Because poor sleep can stem from many underlying causes—stress, hormonal shifts, sleep apnea, or other treatable conditions—a supplement alone may not solve the problem. What Is Melatonin and Why It Matters

Melatonin is a hormone produced by the pineal gland in response to darkness. It helps regulate your sleep–wake cycle:

Signals your body it's time to sleep

Peaks at night and drops in the morning

Can be influenced by light exposure, stress, and age

Many people use synthetic melatonin supplements, but some prefer natural sources. Tart cherries (Prunus cerasus) contain small amounts of melatonin plus antioxidants that may support sleep.

The Science Behind Tart Cherry Juice and Sleep

Several clinical studies have explored whether tart cherry juice can:

Increase melatonin levels

Reduce sleep latency (time to fall asleep)

Improve overall sleep duration and quality
