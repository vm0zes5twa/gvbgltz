# Common vs. Serious Eye Disease: Act Now to Save Your Sight | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

How can I differentiate between common eye diseases and more serious conditions?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Common eye conditions vary widely in severity. Mild issues like conjunctivitis (pink eye) and dry eye syndrome are typically less serious and can often be managed at home with proper hygiene, artificial tears, or over-the-counter remedies. However, warning signs such as sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, flashes of light, or persistent redness may indicate a serious underlying condition and require immediate medical attention.

Because eye symptoms can overlap between minor and urgent conditions, it's difficult to know when to wait it out and when to see a doctor.
