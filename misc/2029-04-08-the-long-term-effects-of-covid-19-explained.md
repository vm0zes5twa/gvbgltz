Brain damage

A lack of sufficient oxygen as a result of severe respiratory issues can greatly impact not only the lungs, but also the brain. People suffering from low oxygen levels can develop both minor and major brain damage, from which they may never recover.

People may also suffer brain damage as a result of stroke or heart attack. Seniors, especially those over age 70, are at much higher risk of these devastating effects, and of long-term effects in general.

Brain damage resulting from Covid-19 infections can cause dementia to manifest later in life; some doctors believe those who survive the virus have an increased risk of developing Alzheimer’s Disease. They believe this is due to the harm of multiple organ symptoms by the virus.

Loss of senses and other effects

Many “long haulers” report a loss of taste and or smell, among other symptoms. For some, these symptoms have returned after weeks or months; others are still hoping to regain these senses in the future.

Erratic pulse and/or blood pressure have also been reported, as well as hair loss and headaches. It is believed that inflammation of the heart may be the root cause of this issue.
