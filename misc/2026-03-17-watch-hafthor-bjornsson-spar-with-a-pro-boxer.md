# Watch Hafthor Bjornsson Spar With a Pro Boxer

Former World's Strongest Man winner Hafthor Bjornsson just shared an update on his training as he continues his journey from the world of strongman events to the boxing ring.

Known to fans as 'The Mountain', a nickname he gained thanks to his role in Game of Thrones and which he kept due to his hulking 450-pound frame, Bjornsson officially announced his retirement from strongman competition in August this year. He is currently in the process of leaning down and working on building speed and stamina on top of his raw strength, in anticipation of his long-awaited grudge match with rival strongman Eddie Hall.

"I'm training four times a week in the mornings, six times a week in the afternoon," he says. "I can feel my endurance increasing, which is good. This morning I did four rounds, three minutes, and what's great there is my opponent was more tired than I was."

"Training with Colin is absolutely amazing, he has a lot of experience in boxing, and it's very good for me to train with him," he says after the session. "I just want to show you guys the progression. I have a great team around me helping me; I'm super super thankful for the guys that are willing to show up every day to help me.
