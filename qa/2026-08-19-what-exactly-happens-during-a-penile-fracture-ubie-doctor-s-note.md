Explanation

During a penile fracture, the penis gets hurt while it is erect, usually from bending too much. This causes a tear in the tunica albuginea, the tissue that helps the penis stay firm.

What Happens:

Injury: The penis is often bent suddenly during activities like intercourse.

Tear: The tunica albuginea, a tough tissue around the spongy part of the penis, tears.

Symptoms: You might hear a popping sensation, feel pain, and notice swelling and bruising.

Treatment:

Surgery: Doctors often need to fix the tear with surgery to prevent problems later.
