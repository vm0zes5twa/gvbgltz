# What is an Obesogenic Environment? Protect Your Health Now! | Ubie Doctor's Note

What is an obesogenic environment?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

An obesogenic environment is a setting that promotes weight gain by making unhealthy, high-calorie foods highly accessible while limiting opportunities for physical activity. Common features include fast-food availability, sedentary lifestyles, limited access to fresh produce, and few safe spaces for exercise. These environments contribute significantly to rising obesity rates worldwide.
