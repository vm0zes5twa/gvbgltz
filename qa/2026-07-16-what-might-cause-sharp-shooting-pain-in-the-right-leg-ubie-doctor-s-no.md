What might cause sharp shooting pain in the right leg?

Reviewed by Courtney Bloomer, DO

Emergency Medicine

Sharp shooting pain in the right leg can be caused by sciatica, which might result from issues like nerve compression or injury, or by complications from medical procedures.

Explanation

Sharp shooting pain in the right leg can have several causes. One common reason is sciatica, which is pain that travels along the sciatic nerve. This nerve runs from the lower back down through the legs. Sciatica can happen due to compression or irritation of this nerve.

Nerve Compression or Injury: Sciatica can be caused by things like a slipped disc or other spinal issues.

Post-Traumatic Sciatica: An injury to the spine can suddenly cause sciatica. This means that if someone hurts their back, it might lead to sharp pain in the leg.
