About the Symptom

Delusions of grandeur can be a false belief that you have more power, worth, knowledge, identity, or special relationships with famous people.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Feel I am special or powerful

Possible Causes

Generally, Megalomania can be related to:

A disorder marked by extreme and persistent shifts in mood that last for weeks to months, from periods of sustained depression to periods of elevated mood and euphoria. The precise cause is unclear, but genetic and environmental factors contribute.

Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) is a condition that is characterized by attention difficulty, hyperactivity, and inability to control one's behavior (impulsiveness). It often starts in childhood and can cause a child to struggle in school, at home, or in social relationships. It is more common in male children. The causes of ADHD remain unclear but risk factors include genetic factors, tobacco use during pregnancy, premature delivery, and low birth weight.

Doctor's Diagnostic Questions

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this symptom:

Do you often feel that you are more important or special than others?

When did you first start experiencing an elevated sense of importance and feeling special?

How has feeling that you are extremely special, important, and powerful impacted your daily activities?
