Explanation

The tip of the urethra may itch for several reasons. This can happen due to several factors, including contact with irritating substances, urinary tract infections (UTIs), sexually transmitted infections (STIs), or a reaction to personal hygiene products. Irritation can also occur from allergic reactions to soaps, lotions, or other chemicals. Sometimes, an infection like yeast, bacteria, or other microorganisms can cause symptoms. In some cases, poor hygiene or wearing tight, non-breathable clothing can contribute to irritation and itching. To help relieve irritation, keep the area clean with warm water and avoid harsh personal care products. If the itching persists, becomes more severe, or you develop other symptoms like pain or unusual discharge, contact a healthcare provider.
