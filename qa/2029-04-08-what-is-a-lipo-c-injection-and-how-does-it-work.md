Lipo C injections are being highly promoted for their potential benefits in weight loss and metabolism enhancement. Potential Side Effects:

Soreness at the Injection Site – One of the most commonly reported side effects is soreness or discomfort where the injection was administered. This experience can vary in intensity but typically resolves within a few days.

Infection at the Injection Site – As with many injections, there is a risk of infection. Proper sterile techniques can minimize this risk, but it cannot be eliminated.

Cardiovascular Risks – Some studies suggest that excessive supplementation of certain amino acids, such as Methionine, may increase levels of homocysteine, a risk factor for heart disease.
