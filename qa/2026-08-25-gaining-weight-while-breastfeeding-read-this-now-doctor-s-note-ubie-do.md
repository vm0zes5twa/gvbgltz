Weight changes during breastfeeding vary from person to person. While breastfeeding burns extra calories and may help some women lose weight, others gain or retain weight due to factors like increased appetite, dietary choices, hormonal shifts, sleep patterns, and activity level. There is no single "normal" outcome—each body responds differently.

Explanation

Breastfeeding affects each woman's weight differently. Some mothers may lose weight because breastfeeding can burn extra calories. However, others might gain or retain weight due to several reasons.

Reasons for Weight Gain or Retention:

Caloric Intake: Some mothers may eat more to meet the increased energy demands of breastfeeding, which can lead to weight gain.

Activity Level: Reduced physical activity after childbirth can contribute to weight retention.

Metabolism: Individual differences in metabolism can affect how quickly a woman loses weight after childbirth.
