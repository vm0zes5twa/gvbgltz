Urgent care centers commonly test for sexually transmitted diseases (STDs), including chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, HIV, herpes, and trichomoniasis. Testing typically involves a urine sample, blood draw, or swab, with results often available within a few days. Urgent care is a convenient option when you have symptoms, possible exposure, or need testing outside of regular doctor's office hours.

However, there are important factors to consider before going. Not every urgent care tests for all STDs, costs and insurance coverage vary, and follow-up care for positive results may be limited. Privacy policies and reporting requirements also differ by location and state law.

Urgent care centers are convenient places to get tested for sexually transmitted diseases (STDs). They usually offer tests for some of the most common infections, such as chlamydia, gonorrhea, and HIV. These centers can be a good choice if you need quick results and do not have easy access to a regular doctor.

Common STD Tests Available:

Chlamydia and Gonorrhea: These are often tested together using a urine sample or a swab.

HIV: Rapid tests are available that can give results in less than 30 minutes.

Getting tested at an urgent care center can help you find out if you have an STD and get the treatment you need quickly.
