How might periods change for women with PCOS as they enter their 30s?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Women with PCOS in their 30s often notice their periods becoming more regular, though symptoms still vary from person to person. Shifting hormone levels and natural aging can both influence menstrual patterns, cycle length, and the intensity of other PCOS-related symptoms like acne, weight changes, or fatigue.

Because PCOS presents differently in every woman—and evolves with age—understanding your unique symptoms is key to getting the right care.
