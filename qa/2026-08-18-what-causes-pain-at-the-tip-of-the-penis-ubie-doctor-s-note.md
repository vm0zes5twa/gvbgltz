Explanation

Pain at the tip of the penis can be caused by various conditions. One possible cause is Peyronie's disease, which involves the development of fibrous scar tissue inside the penis. This can lead to pain and changes in the shape of the penis, such as curvature. Another condition that might cause pain at the tip of the penis is penile induration. This involves the hardening of tissue in the penis, which can also lead to discomfort or pain.
