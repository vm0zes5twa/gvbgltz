Can omega-3 supplements replace statins?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Omega-3 supplements are not a replacement for statins. While both support heart health, they work through different mechanisms and are not equally effective. Statins directly lower LDL ("bad") cholesterol by blocking a liver enzyme involved in cholesterol production, while omega-3s primarily reduce triglycerides and offer anti-inflammatory benefits. Omega-3s have little to no effect on LDL cholesterol and should not be used as a substitute for prescribed statin therapy.
