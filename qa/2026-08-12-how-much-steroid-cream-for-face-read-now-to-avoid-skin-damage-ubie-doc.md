Internal Medicine

Steroid creams can effectively treat facial skin conditions, but proper use is essential. Apply only a thin, pea-sized amount to affected areas, and limit use to short-term treatment unless directed otherwise by a doctor. Misuse or overuse may cause side effects such as skin thinning, redness, acne, rosacea, or steroid-induced dermatitis.

Because facial skin is thinner and more sensitive than skin elsewhere on the body, it absorbs medication more readily—making side effects more likely. If you're unsure whether a steroid cream is right for your symptoms, or you're experiencing irritation, it's worth investigating further before continuing use.
