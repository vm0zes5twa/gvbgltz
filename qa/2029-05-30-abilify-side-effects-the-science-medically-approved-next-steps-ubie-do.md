Abilify Side Effects? The Science & Medically Approved Next Steps

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Abilify (aripiprazole) side effects include common issues like nausea, restlessness, and insomnia, along with less common weight gain, metabolic changes, and movement symptoms. Rare but serious risks include neuroleptic malignant syndrome (NMS), suicidal thoughts, impulse control problems, and low blood pressure.

Key safety steps:

Do not stop Abilify suddenly — taper only under medical guidance.

Track symptoms and discuss dose adjustments or alternatives with your prescriber.

Seek emergency care immediately for high fever with muscle rigidity, fainting, chest pain, seizures, stroke signs, or suicidal thoughts.

Explanation

Abilify (generic name: aripiprazole) is a prescription medication commonly used to treat conditions such as schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, major depressive disorder (as an add-on treatment), irritability associated with autism, and Tourette's disorder. It works by balancing certain brain chemicals, especially dopamine and serotonin.

Like all medications that affect the brain, Abilify can cause side effects. Most are manageable and mild. A small number can be serious. Understanding the difference—and knowing what to do next—can help you stay safe without unnecessary worry.

Below is a clear, medically grounded overview based on clinical research and prescribing data.

Common Abilify Side Effects

Many people tolerate Abilify well. When side effects do occur, they are often mild and improve over time as your body adjusts.

Frequently reported side effects:

Nausea or vomiting

Constipation

Headache

Dizziness or lightheadedness

Trouble sleeping (insomnia)

Anxiety or restlessness

Akathisia (a feeling of inner restlessness and need to move)

Fatigue

Blurred vision

Weight Changes and Metabolism

Compared to some other antipsychotic medications, Abilify has a lower risk of weight gain, but it can still occur. Some people also experience:

Increased blood sugar

Changes in cholesterol levels

Increased appetite

If you're taking Abilify long-term, your doctor may monitor:

Weight

Blood glucose

Lipid levels

These checks are standard and help prevent long-term complications.

Movement-Related Side Effects

Because Abilify affects dopamine, it can sometimes cause movement-related symptoms, known as extrapyramidal symptoms (EPS).

These may include:

Muscle stiffness

Tremors

Slowed movements

Restlessness (akathisia)

Involuntary facial movements

A rare but more persistent condition is tardive dyskinesia, which causes repetitive, involuntary movements (often of the mouth or tongue). The risk increases with long-term use but is generally lower with Abilify than with older antipsychotics.

If you notice unusual movements, speak to your doctor promptly. Early adjustments can often prevent worsening.

2. Suicidal Thoughts (Especially in Younger People)

Like many medications used for depression, Abilify carries a warning about an increased risk of suicidal thoughts in children, teens, and young adults—particularly during the first few months of treatment or after dose changes.

3. Impulse Control Problems

Some people taking Abilify have reported new or increased urges, including:

Gambling

Binge eating

Compulsive shopping

Increased sexual urges

These behaviors are uncommon but documented. If you or a loved one notice sudden behavioral changes, contact your doctor promptly. Adjusting the dose or switching medications often resolves the issue.

4. Low Blood Pressure

Abilify can sometimes cause orthostatic hypotension—meaning your blood pressure drops when you stand up.

Symptoms include:

Dizziness

Fainting

Blurry vision

Standing up slowly and staying hydrated can help, but recurring symptoms should be discussed with your doctor.

Certain individuals may be more vulnerable to Abilify side effects:

Older adults

People with heart disease

Those with diabetes

What To Do If You Experience Side Effects

Mild Side Effects

If symptoms are mild:

Monitor them for a few days

Take the medication at the same time daily

Avoid alcohol

Stay hydrated

Get adequate sleep

Many mild symptoms improve as your body adjusts.

Moderate Side Effects

If side effects interfere with daily life:

Contact your prescribing doctor

Do not stop Abilify abruptly

Ask about dose adjustments

Discuss alternative medications if needed

Stopping Abilify suddenly can cause withdrawal-like symptoms or worsening of the underlying condition.

Severe or Life-Threatening Symptoms

Seek immediate emergency care if you experience:

High fever with muscle rigidity

Severe confusion

Chest pain

Seizures

Signs of stroke (sudden weakness, slurred speech)

Suicidal thoughts

Then follow up directly with your prescribing doctor.

How Doctors Reduce Risk When Prescribing Abilify

Medical professionals follow established guidelines to minimize side effects. This typically includes:

Starting at the lowest effective dose

Gradually increasing if needed

Monitoring weight and labs

Screening for personal and family history of metabolic disease

Reviewing other medications for interactions

Never adjust your dose without medical guidance.

Alternative treatment options

For some people, Abilify is life-changing and stabilizes severe psychiatric symptoms. For others, side effects outweigh benefits. This is a highly individual decision and should always involve your doctor.

Abruptly stopping Abilify can lead to:

Mood destabilization

Return of psychosis

Insomnia

A gradual taper is usually safest.

Balanced Perspective: Risk vs Benefit

It's important not to let lists of potential side effects create unnecessary fear.

Every FDA-approved medication—including Abilify—has undergone extensive clinical testing. Serious side effects are rare. For many individuals, the benefit of stabilizing mood, reducing psychosis, or improving daily functioning far outweighs the risks.

The key is monitoring and communication.

Final Thoughts

Abilify is an effective and widely prescribed medication, but like all medications that affect brain chemistry, it can cause side effects. Most are manageable. A small number are serious and require urgent care.

The safest course of action is always to:

Monitor your symptoms carefully

Avoid stopping medication suddenly

Speak to a doctor about anything that could be life-threatening or serious

If you are ever unsure, err on the side of caution and seek medical evaluation.

Your health and safety come first—and open communication with your healthcare provider is the best way to ensure both.
