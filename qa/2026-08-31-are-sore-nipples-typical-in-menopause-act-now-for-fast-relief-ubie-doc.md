Sore or tender nipples can be a symptom of menopause, though it's less common than hot flashes or mood changes. During perimenopause and menopause, fluctuating estrogen and progesterone levels can cause breast tissue sensitivity, tingling, or nipple soreness. These hormonal shifts affect the milk ducts and connective tissue, sometimes leading to discomfort similar to what many experience before a menstrual period. While usually harmless, persistent nipple pain should be evaluated to rule out other causes.
