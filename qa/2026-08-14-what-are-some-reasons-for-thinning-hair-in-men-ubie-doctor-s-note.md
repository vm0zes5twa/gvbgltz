Male Pattern Baldness: This is the most frequent reason for hair loss in men. It is largely genetic and involves a gradual thinning of hair on the scalp, often starting at the temples and crown.

Aging: As men age, hair naturally becomes thinner and may not grow back as quickly. This is a normal part of the aging process.

Other Health Issues: Sometimes, thinning hair can be a sign of other health problems or conditions, such as hormonal changes or nutritional deficiencies.
